Ta-ti-ti

Rhythm as a toy: listen, tap it yourself, build your own. One metronome click is one “ta”.

Notes on one line, like in the book. The “With blocks” view also shows how long each sound lasts, like the piano roll in GarageBand. Numbers are the count; “&” falls exactly between clicks.

On iPhone, turn off silent mode, or there will be no sound. On a computer, tap with Space or Enter; Esc stops.

Build your own

Tap the pieces and they fill the bars in order. The longer the piece, the longer it sounds. The first tap clears the track. With “Endless”, your rhythm loops.
